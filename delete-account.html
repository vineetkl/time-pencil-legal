Delete your Time Pencil account

For Time Pencil by Vineet Kapil · iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android

You only have a Time Pencil account if you chose to sign in (with Apple, Google or an email code). Using the app without signing in creates no account.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Time Pencil and go to Settings → Backup.
  2. Open your account and tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

Or ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app, email vineetkapilm@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Time Pencil account”. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, sign-in method and account ID.
  • Everything synced to it: blocks you drew, notes, Year plans and settings.

The copy on your devices stays there until you delete the app. Your calendar events were never stored by us, so they are not affected. Bug reports you sent are kept but no longer linked to you. Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google; deleting your account does not cancel one, so cancel it in your store account settings.

Privacy Policy

For the Time Pencil apps on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac, Android and Wear OS · Last updated 31 October 2026

Time Pencil (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is designed to keep your data on your device. You can use it fully without an account. This policy explains what the app accesses, what leaves your device, who receives it, and why. By using the app you agree to this policy.

On your device only

  • Calendars and Reminders. With your permission, the app reads and writes events in the calendars on your device (including iCloud, Google or Outlook accounts you have added there) so it can show your day on the clock and let you create or edit events. Your calendar events are never uploaded to us, even if you sign in. Each device reads its own calendars.
  • Your clock: blocks you draw, notes, Year plans, colours and settings are stored on your device. They leave it only if you sign in to sync (see below).
  • Widgets, Apple Watch, Wear OS and Live Activities get their data from the app on your own devices.
  • Notifications and alarms are scheduled on your device. We do not use push notifications.

Optional account and sync

If you choose to sign in (with Apple, with Google, or with a code sent to your email), we store:

  • your email address, the sign-in method you used, and an account ID;
  • the blocks you draw, notes, Year plans and settings, so they sync between your devices. Calendar events are not included.

This data is stored with Supabase, our database provider (Supabase privacy policy). Apple or Google confirm your identity when you sign in with them. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising.

Deleting your account: you can delete your account in the app (Settings → Backup → your account → Delete account), or ask us to on the account deletion page. This permanently deletes your account and everything synced to it.

Live sky and weather

If you turn on Live sky and allow location, the app uses your location on your device to place the sun and sky. To show the current weather, it sends an approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) to the weather service of MET Norway (api.met.no). If you also turn on live webcam skies with your own Windy key, the same approximate location is sent to Windy (windy.com) to find a nearby webcam. We do not store your location. You can turn location off in your device settings at any time.

Purchases

Purchases (the optional Pro subscription and lifetime unlock) are processed by Apple and Google. We use RevenueCat to verify and manage them. RevenueCat receives your purchase and receipt details together with an anonymous app user ID, or, if you are signed in, your account ID so Pro follows your account to your other devices. RevenueCat does not receive your name or payment card details, and we never receive your payment information. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy. If you redeem an invite or gift code, we record that the code was used by your account so it cannot be used again.

Advertising measurement (iPhone and iPad)

The iPhone and iPad app includes Meta’s (Facebook’s) SDK for one purpose: to tell Meta when the app is installed and opened, so we can see whether our ads on Instagram and Facebook work. It is set up so that:

  • the app never asks for permission to track you and does not share your device’s advertising identifier;
  • Meta receives only the install or open event with basic technical details (such as device model, iOS version, language, time zone and an app-generated anonymous ID), plus crash reports from Meta’s own code;
  • Meta receives nothing about your calendar, your plans, your notes or anything you draw, and nothing about your purchases;
  • installs that come from ads are reported through Apple’s privacy-preserving ad attribution, which does not identify you.

See Meta’s Privacy Policy. The Android, Mac, Apple Watch and Wear OS apps do not include it.

Bug reports and feedback

If you send a bug report from the app, we receive what you write, a screenshot if you choose to attach one, and basic diagnostics: device type and system version, app version, language, screen size, the number (not the content) of your blocks and notes, whether you have Pro, and recent error messages. If you are signed in, the report is linked to your account so we can follow up. Reports are stored with Supabase and used only to fix problems. If you delete your account, reports you sent are kept but no longer linked to you. Feedback forms open in your browser on Google Forms, where Google’s privacy policy applies.

Update check

On iPhone and iPad, the app asks Apple’s App Store for the latest version number so it can tell you when an update is available. No personal data is sent.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not use analytics services. We do not collect your contacts or photos. Apart from the Meta install measurement described above, we do not share data with advertisers, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices and rights

You can use the app without signing in. You can revoke calendar, reminders and location access at any time in your device settings, sign out, or delete your account in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. To ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion, email us at the address below.
